Today was the last day of the [NC Bird Atlas](https://ncbirdatlas.org) data collection. While the work is not over, what a milestone! Staff and volunteers collected over 4.7 million observations since January of 2021.

{% include image.html src="/img/2026-02-28-ncba-last-day.jpg" alt="Three staff members standing in a field on a sunny day" caption="Lee Sherrill, Charlie Ayers, and Sam Payne squeeze in a few last checklists on the last day of NC Bird Atlas data collection." %}

Next, [NC Wildlife Resources Commission](https://ncwildlife.gov) staff will begin work on building a coalition to develop final products for the Atlas.
