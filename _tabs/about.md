North Carolina Partners in Flight is a cooperative effort involving government agencies, conservation organizations, private industry, the academic community and citizens to further bird conservation.  The primary focus of the program is to coordinate and promote education, habitat conservation and research efforts among partners.

NC Partners in Flight is an open and informal group of those interested in advancing bird conservation, education, and research. Scott Anderson, Bird Conservation Biologist at the NC Wildlife Resources Commission is the current coordinator.
